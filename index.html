Privacy Policy

Capybara Fishing RNG · Moss Roof Studio · Last updated 20 October 2026

Capybara Fishing RNG (“the game”) is published on CrazyGames. This page explains what data the game collects and why.

What we collect

  • A random player ID created by the game. It is stored in your game save and is not linked to your name, e-mail or CrazyGames account.
  • Gameplay events: tutorial steps, features you use (for example daily rewards and seasonal events), loading times and technical errors.
  • Technical data our analytics provider receives automatically: device type, operating system, browser, game version, session start and length, and approximate country derived from your IP address.

Why we collect it

Only to understand how the game is played and to improve it: where new players get stuck, which features bring players back and which bugs need fixing. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not build profiles of individual players.

Who processes it

Analytics is provided by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark), which processes the data on our behalf: gameanalytics.com/privacy. CrazyGames processes data under its own policy: crazygames.com/privacy-policy.

How long we keep it

As long as GameAnalytics retains it for our account, and no longer than needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the data linked to your play. Write to drtabaluga780@gmail.com and describe your device, browser and the dates you played. Clearing your browser data or game save starts a new random player ID.

Children

The game does not ask for, and does not knowingly collect, information that identifies a child.
